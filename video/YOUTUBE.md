# YouTube upload

## Title

Fenestella: the small window on a QoreChain account (Build Before Q-Day demo)

## Description

Fenestella shows what a stolen classical key could still do to a QoreChain account, opens an EVM window scoped to exactly what you need, and lets an AI agent pay bills on a leash the chain enforces.

Recorded on the live site and the QoreChain testnet (qorechain-diana). Every result in the video is a real testnet transaction, listed in the repository.
