## Title

Fenestella: an AI agent that pays your bills and can't rob you (QoreChain, Build Before Q-Day)

## Description

Fenestella shows what a stolen classical key could still do to a QoreChain account, opens an EVM window scoped to exactly what you need, and puts an AI agent on a leash the chain enforces. The agent's AI reads invoices, checks the chain with its own tools, refuses a phishing invoice, and pays a real bill only from a mandate sealed with ML-KEM-1024 and signed with your post-quantum key. A stolen agent key is refused by the chain.

Recorded on the live site and the QoreChain testnet (qorechain-diana). Every result in the video is a real testnet transaction, listed in the repository.
